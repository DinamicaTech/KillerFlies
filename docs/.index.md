## (root) · Killer Flies
- File: README.md
- Children: architecture, creative-lab, enemigos, global-rules, graficos, jugador, marcador, multijugador, partida, power-ups, sonido

A remake of the classic arcade game Galaxian: the player's ship moves along the bottom of the screen and shoots down waves of alien ships that dive at it while dropping bombs.
The formation moves from side to side and launches attacks; each destroyed wave gives way to a faster one, with a different formation and mix of ships: the four arcade types are joined by three with attacks of their own (zigzag, ramming and sniper).
Destroying a diving yellow ship together with its escort drops a random power-up. The player has three ships; the scoreboard shows the wave and the points next to their high scores. It is played in the browser with the keyboard or, on a phone, with the touch screen and by tilting it.
The project is also a public demo of how Claude Visual Project works, so all its docs are written in English. Two players can also play together online, each on their own device, joining with a room code or a QR.

## architecture · Architecture
- File: architecture.md
- Depends on: jugador, partida, sonido

Technical decisions that shape the whole game: it is played in the browser, in JavaScript with no build step, with graphics generated by code and sounds from recorded audio files kept in the repository, high scores saved in the browser, keyboard, touch screen (swipe and short tap) or phone tilt controls and published on GitHub Pages (https://dinamicatech.github.io/KillerFlies/), although it still works by opening index.html locally. The game's common base (index.html, screen, game loop, keyboard, touch screen and phone tilt) is in js/base.js; the screen measures 224×288 logical pixels and is drawn at four times that resolution. The online two-player game connects the two browsers directly (WebRTC with PeerJS, copied into js/lib) and only uses PeerJS's free server to introduce them.

## enemigos · Enemies
- File: enemigos/README.md
- Depends on: graficos
- Children: enemigos/ataque, enemigos/formacion

The alien ships: seven types, each with its points and its maximum bombs per attack: blue (10, 2), purple (20, 3), red (30, 4) and yellow (50, 4), the arcade ones, and three that appear from wave 2 with their own attack: green (40, 2, zigzag), orange (60, no bombs, rams) and cyan (80, 3, shooter). A ship dies when hit by a player shot or when it collides with the player: it explodes on the spot and sends a notification so its points get added. Its child nodes define the formations of each wave and the attacks.

## enemigos/ataque · Attack
- File: enemigos/ataque.md
- Depends on: enemigos, enemigos/formacion, jugador, graficos, architecture

The attacks: a ship (or a yellow with its escort of reds) leaves the formation with a small parabola, turns, dives toward the player dropping bombs and, if it does not collide, exits at the bottom, reappears at the top and returns to its place. The new types come down in their own way: the green in zigzag, the orange rams in a straight line without bombs and the cyan stops at mid-screen and fires an aimed burst. One or two attacks start at a time and there are more with each wave, which also makes them faster. When the player's spare ship comes out, attacks wait two seconds. The yellow leaves with the two reds below it (or the nearest ones); if the yellow is destroyed during the attack, its reds stop dropping bombs. An attacking ship that collides with the player makes it explode and is destroyed; the player's shot also hits it.

## enemigos/formacion · Formation
- File: enemigos/formacion.md
- Depends on: enemigos, architecture, graficos, enemigos/ataque, partida

The block of alien ships at the top of the screen: its layout on a grid of 6 rows by 10 columns, different in each wave, and its horizontal movement from one side to the other. There are six formations (Clásica (Classic), Uve (V), Rombo (Diamond), Columnas (Columns), Ajedrez (Chessboard) and Fortaleza (Fortress)) that change the shape and the mix of the seven ship types; wave 1 is the arcade's classic one and from wave 7 onwards formations 2 to 6 repeat in order. The block turns around when the outermost living ship touches the edge and starts out crossing the screen in about 4 seconds; each ship keeps its slot so it can return to it after an attack.

## jugador · Player
- File: jugador.md
- Depends on: enemigos/formacion, graficos, architecture
- Children: jugador/accelerometer-control, jugador/destello-disparo, jugador/escudo-al-aparecer, jugador/estela-motor, jugador/explosion-fragmentos, jugador/inercia, jugador/temblor-al-morir

The ship the player controls at the bottom of the screen: it moves horizontally with the arrow keys, by sliding a finger or by tilting the phone (jugador/accelerometer-control), fires with the space bar or a short tap on the touch screen, one shot at a time (the shot is freed when it hits a ship, in formation or diving, or when it leaves through the top; the active power-up changes this), and explodes if a ship or a bomb hits it. It does not respawn by itself: Game makes it appear in the center when the game starts and with each spare ship, and outside a game it is not there. When it appears it blinks for two seconds and is invulnerable meanwhile (jugador/escudo-al-aparecer). When it fires, the cannon gives a brief flash (jugador/destello-disparo). When it explodes it throws out fragments that scatter and fade out (jugador/explosion-fragmentos). It accelerates and brakes smoothly, at 90 pixels per second at most with the arrows and 120 on a phone (jugador/inercia). While it moves, a small flame flickers under it (jugador/estela-motor). Improvement pending in a child draft: shake on death. In a two-player game (multijugador) there are two ships, J1 and J2 in different colours, each with its own power-up, spare ships and points.

## jugador/accelerometer-control · Accelerometer control
- File: jugador/accelerometer-control.md
- Depends on: architecture, partida, multijugador

On a phone, the player's ship also moves by tilting the device sideways: up to 3° of tilt it stays still, and from there its speed grows with the tilt up to 120 pixels per second at 30°, with the same inertia as the other controls (jugador/inercia). Tilt and finger work together without choosing a mode: while an arrow is pressed or a finger is sliding (or the ship is still reaching where the finger took it), the tilt is ignored. The position of the phone when the game starts, or when it resumes after a pause (partida/pausa), counts as the centre. Architecture reads the sensor (KF.inclinacion in js/base.js; on iPhone it asks for permission with the first short tap); Game calibrates the centre on starting and, in a two-player game, the guest calibrates it when its game starts. It came from the creative lab.

## multijugador · Multiplayer
- File: multijugador.md
- Depends on: partida, jugador, marcador, architecture, enemigos, enemigos/formacion, enemigos/ataque, power-ups, sonido, graficos

Online two-player game, each player on their own device, in js/red.js: the start menu offers '1 JUGADOR', 'CREAR PARTIDA' and 'UNIRSE'. Creating a game opens a room with a 4-letter code and shows it with a QR of the game link (?sala=CODE); the other player scans the QR or types the code under 'UNIRSE'. The two browsers connect directly (WebRTC with PeerJS, vendored in js/lib; PeerJS's free server only introduces them). The host (J1) runs the whole game and sends a snapshot 30 times per second; the guest (J2) draws it interpolated 80 ms behind and moves its own ship at once, sending its position and the shots it asks for. Cooperative: each player has 3 ships, their own points (J1 and J2 in their ship's colour) and their own power-up; on each device the other player's ship is drawn as a semi-transparent ghost so each player can tell their own ship apart; the game ends when neither has ships. If a player stops being heard the game pauses for up to 30 seconds; then the host carries on alone and the guest returns to the menu. Either player can pause the game for both (partida/pausa); only the one who paused resumes it.

## partida · Game
- File: partida.md
- Depends on: jugador, enemigos, enemigos/formacion, enemigos/ataque, marcador, graficos
- Children: partida/pausa

The game cycle, in js/partida.js: a 'START GAME' screen with the formation moving behind it and a menu ('1 JUGADOR', 'CREAR PARTIDA', 'UNIRSE'; any key or a short tap starts a one-player game, as before); successive waves, each 5% faster than the previous one in everything (block, dives and bombs); three player ships (one in play and two spares, which the extra life power-up can raise up to five) and 'GAME OVER' on losing the last one, which stays until a key is pressed or a short tap is made and then returns to 'START GAME'. It tells the scoreboard the wave; each player ship carries its spare ships and points. In a two-player game (multijugador) each player has their own ships and the game ends when neither has any. A game can be paused while playing (partida/pausa).

## partida/pausa · Pause
- File: partida/pausa.md
- Depends on: partida, multijugador, sonido, jugador/accelerometer-control, architecture

Pausing a game, in js/pausa.js (KF.pausa): the P key, or a tap on the small II button at the top centre of touch screens, pauses and resumes; while paused any short tap resumes without shooting. The game also pauses by itself when the page is hidden (tab change, minimised window or locked phone) and never resumes by itself. Everything freezes, the sound goes silent and 'PAUSA' is shown in the centre; on resuming, the phone's position becomes the centre of the tilt again. In a two-player game both devices pause, show 'PAUSA J1' or 'PAUSA J2', and only the player who paused can resume. It came from the creative lab and is validated by the owner.

## power-ups · Power-ups
- File: power-ups.md
- Depends on: jugador, enemigos, enemigos/formacion, enemigos/ataque, graficos, sonido, partida
- Children: power-ups/aspecto-nave

Random power-ups, in js/powerups.js: when a yellow and its two red escorts are destroyed outside the formation, a blinking capsule falls at half the speed of a bomb (the same for every power and worth no points). If the player ship picks it up, it gets a random power, all with the same probability: rapid shot, deep shot, bomb, annihilation (single use), slow fire (a power-down) or triple shot, or else an extra life (one more spare ship, up to 5). It lasts until the end of the wave, a new one replaces the previous one, and it is lost if the ship is destroyed. To test them, Shift+1…7 gives each power from the keyboard. Each pickup, extra life included, is counted in KF.powerups.recogidas so Sound can play its sound. In a two-player game the power belongs to whoever picks up the capsule.
