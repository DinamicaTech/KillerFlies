## (root) · Killer Flies
- File: README.md
- Children: architecture, creative-lab, enemigos, global-rules, graficos, jugador, marcador, multijugador, partida, power-ups, sonido

A remake of the classic arcade game Galaxian: the player's ship moves along the bottom of the screen and shoots down waves of alien ships that dive at it while dropping bombs.
The formation moves from side to side and launches attacks; each destroyed wave gives way to a faster one, with a different formation and mix of ships: the four arcade types are joined by three with attacks of their own (zigzag, ramming and sniper).
Every third wave ends with a mothership boss that takes many hits. Destroying a diving yellow ship together with its escort drops a random power-up. The player has three ships and earns one more every 10,000 points; the scoreboard shows the wave and the points next to their high scores. It is played in the browser with the keyboard or, on a phone, with the touch screen and by tilting it.
The project is also a public demo of how Claude Visual Project works, so all its docs are written in English. Two players can also play together online, each on their own device, joining with a room code or a QR.

## architecture · Architecture
- File: architecture.md
- Depends on: jugador, partida, sonido
- Children: architecture/app-instalable

Technical decisions that shape the whole game: it is played in the browser, in JavaScript with no build step, with graphics generated by code and sounds from recorded audio files kept in the repository, high scores saved in the browser, keyboard, touch screen (swipe and short tap) or phone tilt controls and published on GitHub Pages (https://dinamicatech.github.io/KillerFlies/), from where it can be installed on the phone as an app that runs full screen and offline (architecture/app-instalable), although it still works by opening index.html locally. The game's common base (index.html, screen, game loop, keyboard, touch screen and phone tilt) is in js/base.js; the screen measures 224×288 logical pixels and is drawn at four times that resolution. The online two-player game connects the two browsers directly (WebRTC with PeerJS, copied into js/lib) and only uses PeerJS's free server to introduce them.

## architecture/app-instalable · Installable app
- File: architecture/app-instalable.md
- Depends on: architecture

The game can be installed on the phone's home screen as an app (a PWA): on Android from Chrome's menu ("Install app"), on iPhone from Safari's Share → "Add to Home Screen"; there is no install button inside the game. Its icon is the player's ship on a starry black background. Opened from the icon it runs in full screen and portrait, with no browser bars (on Android with no status bar either; on iPhone the status bar stays, Apple allows no more). The first visit with a connection saves the whole game on the device, so the one-player game, sounds included, is then played offline; the two-player game still needs a connection. With a connection each file is always asked to the server, so the latest published version is played. Opening index.html from disk works as before (no app, no offline copy). On iPhone the installed app keeps its own high scores, apart from Safari's; on Android they are shared. Validated by the owner.

Files: manifest.webmanifest (name, full screen, portrait, icons), sw.js (service worker: saves the game and serves it offline), js/instalable.js (registers sw.js, only over http or https), the icons in assets/iconos (generated from the game's own ship drawing with tools/generar-iconos.html) and the check tools/comprobar-instalable.mjs, run by the "Pruebas" workflow on every pull request, every push to main, weekly and on demand.

Acceptance: on an Android phone, open https://dinamicatech.github.io/KillerFlies/, install it from Chrome's menu, open it from its icon (full screen, portrait, no bars), switch on airplane mode, close and reopen it and play a whole game with sound.

The alien ships: seven types, each with its points and its maximum bombs per attack: blue (10, 2), purple (20, 3), red (30, 4) and yellow (50, 4), the arcade ones, and three that appear from wave 2 with their own attack: green (40, 2, zigzag), orange (60, no bombs, rams) and cyan (80, 3, shooter). ## enemigos/ataque · Attack
- File: enemigos/ataque.md
- Depends on: enemigos, enemigos/formacion, jugador, graficos, architecture

The attacks: a ship (or a yellow with its escort of reds) leaves the formation with a small parabola, turns, dives toward the player dropping bombs and, if it does not collide, exits at the bottom, reappears at the top and returns to its place. The new types come down in their own way: the green in zigzag, the orange rams in a straight line without bombs and the cyan stops at mid-screen and fires an aimed burst. One or two attacks start at a time and there are more with each wave, which also makes them faster; the difficulty level (partida/dificultad) takes one away in EASY and adds one in HARD. When the player's spare ship comes out, attacks wait two seconds. The yellow leaves with the two reds below it (or the nearest ones); if the yellow is destroyed during the attack, its reds stop dropping bombs. An attacking ship that collides with the player makes it explode and is destroyed; the player's shot also hits it.

- File: enemigos/formacion.md

The block of alien ships at the top of the screen: its layout on a grid of 6 rows by 10 columns, different in each wave, and its horizontal movement from one side to the other. There are six formations (Clásica (Classic), Uve (V), Rombo (Diamond), Columnas (Columns), Ajedrez (Chessboard) and Fortaleza (Fortress)) that change the shape and the mix of the seven ship types; wave 1 is the arcade's classic one and from wave 7 onwards formations 2 to 6 repeat in order. ## enemigos/jefe · Mothership boss
- File: enemigos/jefe.md
- Depends on: enemigos, enemigos/ataque, partida, partida/oleada-desafio, jugador, power-ups, marcador/combos, graficos, sonido, multijugador

The mothership boss, in js/jefe.js (KF.jefe): after clearing every third wave (3, 6, 9…), before the challenge wave, 'MOTHERSHIP' is shown for 2 seconds while a large mothership (44×22 pixels, about four ships wide) comes down alone, with no formation. It then moves from side to side near the top and fires bursts of 3 bombs in a fan aimed at the nearest player ship, every 2 seconds, faster with each wave like everything else; it holds its fire while no player ship is in play, so the spare ship can come out. It takes 20 hits the first time, 30 the second, 40 the third and so on; a health bar above it shows what is left and each hit makes it flash white and play the alien explosion sound. Every player shot that touches it stops there (the deep shot included) and takes 1; the bomb and the annihilation power-ups take 5. When destroyed it explodes in a chain of explosions for 2 seconds and gives 2,000 points per appearance (2,000, 4,000, 6,000…), multiplied by the combo, to whoever lands the last hit; then the challenge wave and the next wave follow. Meanwhile no capsules fall, the active power-up is kept and the background music plays at its starting speed. In a two-player game the host runs it and sends the guest its time, phase and health; the guest computes its position. Validated by the owner.

Acceptance: after clearing wave 3 the mothership appears, falls after 20 hits and adds 2,000 points; then come the challenge wave and wave 4; the next mothership comes after wave 6 with 30 hits and 4,000 points.

On a phone, the player's ship also moves by tilting the device sideways: up to 3° of tilt it stays still, and from there its speed grows with the tilt up to 120 pixels per second at 30°, with the same inertia as the other controls (jugador/inercia). ## marcador/combos · Combos
- File: marcador/combos.md
- Depends on: marcador, jugador, enemigos, partida, partida/oleada-desafio, power-ups, multijugador, graficos

Points multiplier for destroying ships in a row without missing, in js/combos.js (KF.combos): each player ship keeps its streak (KF.Nave.racha). The first 5 ships in a row give their usual points, the 6th to the 10th double, the 11th to the 15th triple and from the 16th on quadruple. The streak goes back to zero when a shot leaves through the top without hitting anything (with the triple shot, only if none of its three projectiles hits) and when the player ship explodes. It also counts in the challenge wave and carries over from one wave to the next. Ships destroyed by collision, the bomb, the annihilation or the deep shot count like any other, and so does the mothership boss (enemigos/jefe), with its own points; a hit on it is not a lost shot. The points earned, already multiplied, float where each ship explodes: they rise 10 pixels and fade out in 0.8 seconds, in white (in a two-player game, in each player's colour). While the multiplier is active the scoreboard shows 'X2' to 'X4' under the points (in a two-player game, to the left of each player's points). In a two-player game the host computes the combos and sends the guest each player's streak and the floating points.

## multijugador · Multiplayer
- File: multijugador.md
- Depends on: partida, jugador, marcador, architecture, enemigos, enemigos/formacion, enemigos/ataque, power-ups, sonido, graficos

Online two-player game, each player on their own device, in js/red.js: the start menu offers '1 PLAYER', 'CREATE GAME' and 'JOIN GAME'. Creating a game opens a room with a 4-letter code and shows it with a QR of the game link (?room=CODE); the other player scans the QR or types the code under 'JOIN GAME'. The two browsers connect directly (WebRTC with PeerJS, vendored in js/lib; PeerJS's free server only introduces them). The host (J1) runs the whole game, the challenge wave (partida/oleada-desafio) and the mothership boss (enemigos/jefe) included, and sends a snapshot 30 times per second; the guest (J2) draws it interpolated 80 ms behind and moves its own ship at once, sending its position and the shots it asks for. Cooperative: each player has 3 ships, their own points (J1 and J2 in their ship's colour) and their own power-up; on each device the other player's ship is drawn as a semi-transparent ghost so each player can tell their own ship apart; the game ends when neither has ships. If a player stops being heard the game pauses for up to 30 seconds; then the host carries on alone and the guest returns to the menu. Either player can pause the game for both (partida/pausa); only the one who paused resumes it or chooses 'RESTART', which starts a new game for both as 'PLAY AGAIN' does. After 'GAME OVER' the two stay connected: back in the start menu, 'PLAY AGAIN' starts a new game for both with the same P1 and P2, without a new room. All on-screen texts are in English and the players are shown as P1 and P2.

## partida · Game

The game cycle, in js/partida.js: a 'START GAME' screen with the formation moving behind it and a menu ('1 PLAYER', 'CREATE GAME', 'JOIN GAME', and first 'PLAY AGAIN' after a two-player game while both players are still connected; any key or a short tap starts the selected option, '1 PLAYER' by default; under the options, the difficulty line '< NORMAL >', see partida/dificultad); successive waves with a challenge wave between one and the next (partida/oleada-desafio) and, after every third wave (3, 6, 9…), the mothership boss before it (enemigos/jefe), each wave 5% faster than the previous one in everything (block, dives and bombs), all of them multiplied by the difficulty level (partida/dificultad); three player ships (one in play and two spares, which the extra life power-up, the perfect challenge wave and every 10,000 points (partida/vida-extra-por-puntos) can raise up to five) and 'GAME OVER' on losing the last one, which stays until a key is pressed or a short tap is made and then returns to 'START GAME' (in a one-player game, through the hall of fame, marcador/hall-of-fame). ## partida/dificultad · Difficulty levels
- File: partida/dificultad.md
- Depends on: partida, enemigos/ataque, enemigos/formacion, enemigos/jefe, graficos, multijugador

The player chooses EASY, NORMAL or HARD in the start menu: a line '< NORMAL >' under the menu options, changed with the left and right arrows or with a tap on it (each tap goes to the next level). The choice is remembered in the browser; with nothing saved it is NORMAL. NORMAL is the game as it was before the levels. EASY makes everything 25% slower (formation block, dives, bombs, power-up capsules and the mothership boss), with one attacker less at a time (never fewer than one) and 30% more time between attacks; HARD makes everything 25% faster, with one attacker more at a time and 25% less time between attacks. The 5% increase per wave, the spare ships, the extra lives and the points are the same in every level, and there is a single high score for all of them. The formation behind the menu moves at the speed of the chosen level. In a two-player game the host's level applies to both: it reaches the guest in the snapshot. In js/dificultad.js (KF.dificultad: `elegido` is the level marked in the menu, `nivel` the one of the game in play); Game multiplies each wave's speed by it and Attack takes its attackers and pauses from it. Tested by tools/comprobar-dificultad.mjs on every pull request. Validated by the owner.

## partida/empezar-tras-perfect · Start after a perfect
- File: partida/empezar-tras-perfect.md
- Status: draft

In a one-player game, clearing a main wave without losing any ship while it lasts (a 'perfect') saves in the browser, for that difficulty, a starting point: the next wave with the points the player had when its last ship was destroyed (the best points are kept if the perfect is repeated). ## partida/oleada-desafio · Challenge wave
- File: partida/oleada-desafio.md
- Status: draft
- Depends on: enemigos, enemigos/formacion, jugador, marcador, power-ups, multijugador, sonido

The challenge wave, in js/desafio.js (KF.desafio): after clearing each wave, before the next one, 'CHALLENGE' is shown for 2 seconds and then four groups of six ships (blue, purple, green, red) cross the screen one after another along fixed curved paths (a loop from the top left, the same mirrored, a wave from left to right and a U-turn from the right), a group every 4 seconds, each ship taking 6 seconds to cross; about 20 seconds in all. In a two-player game the host runs it and sends the guest its time and which ships are alive; the guest computes their positions. Pending the owner's validation.

## partida/pausa · Pause
- File: partida/pausa.md
- Depends on: partida, multijugador, sonido, sonido/ajustes-sonido, jugador/accelerometer-control, architecture

Pausing a game, in js/pausa.js (KF.pausa): the P key, or a tap on the small II button at the top centre of touch screens, pauses and resumes. While paused, 'PAUSED' is shown with four options under it: 'CONTINUE' (marked), 'MUSIC' and 'SOUNDS' with their volume (sonido/ajustes-sonido) and 'RESTART', which starts a new game from where the one being left started (wave 1, or its perfect starting point, partida/empezar-tras-perfect); the up and down arrows change the option and Enter or space choose it, choosing MUSIC or SOUNDS lowers its volume one step, and a tap on an option chooses it while a tap elsewhere continues, without shooting. The game also pauses by itself when the page is hidden (tab change, minimised window or locked phone) and never resumes by itself. Everything freezes and the sound goes silent; on resuming, the phone's position becomes the centre of the tilt again. In a two-player game both devices pause, show 'PAUSED P1' or 'PAUSED P2', and only the player who paused sees the options and chooses; 'RESTART' starts a new game for both, as 'PLAY AGAIN' does, which helps when one of them has lost all their ships. It came from the creative lab and is validated by the owner, including the CONTINUE and RESTART menu.

## power-ups · Power-ups
- File: power-ups.md
- Depends on: jugador, enemigos, enemigos/formacion, enemigos/ataque, graficos, sonido, partida
- Children: power-ups/aspecto-nave

Random power-ups, in js/powerups.js: when a yellow and its two red escorts are destroyed outside the formation, a blinking capsule falls at half the speed of a bomb (the same for every power and worth no points). If the player ship picks it up, it gets a random power, all with the same probability: rapid shot, deep shot, bomb, annihilation (single use), slow fire (a power-down) or triple shot, or else an extra life (one more spare ship, up to 5). It lasts until the end of the wave (the challenge wave that follows does not end it; its ships are also hit by the bomb and annihilation), the mothership boss does not end it either (enemigos/jefe), a new one replaces the previous one, and it is lost if the ship is destroyed. To test them, Shift+1…7 gives each power from the keyboard. Each pickup, extra life included, is counted in KF.powerups.recogidas so Sound can play its sound. In a two-player game the power belongs to whoever picks up the capsule.

## sonido · Sound
- File: sonido.md
- Depends on: architecture, jugador, enemigos, enemigos/ataque
- Children: sonido/ajustes-sonido, sonido/sonidos-con-profundidad

The game's sounds, recorded audio files played with Web Audio from js/sonido.js (the detail is in sonido/sonidos-con-profundidad): the shot, the explosions of alien ships (a different one for the yellow), the player's explosion, a sound while alien ships attack, a sound on each power-up, a tune at the start of each wave and background music that speeds up as fewer ships remain (at its starting speed during the challenge wave, partida/oleada-desafio, and the mothership boss, enemigos/jefe). Each hit on the mothership plays the alien explosion and its destruction the yellow's. Sound is enabled with the first key pressed or the first touch on the screen, and goes silent while the page is hidden or the game is paused (partida/pausa). The music and the other sounds have separate volumes, set in the pause menu (sonido/ajustes-sonido).

## sonido/ajustes-sonido · Sound settings
- File: sonido/ajustes-sonido.md
- Depends on: sonido, partida/pausa, graficos

The player sets the volume of the music and of the sound effects separately, in the pause menu: 'MUSIC 100%' and 'SOUNDS 100%' between 'CONTINUE' and 'RESTART'. Each goes 100%, 75%, 50%, 25% or OFF; choosing it (Enter, space or a tap) lowers it one step and from OFF goes back to 100%, and the left and right arrows lower or raise it without wrapping. MUSIC is the background music and the wave-start tune; SOUNDS is everything else (shot, explosions, attack sound, power-up). Both are remembered in the browser; with nothing saved they are at 100%. In js/sonido.js (KF.sonido.niveles, cambiarVolumen) with one gain per group; the menu is in js/pausa.js. Tested by tools/comprobar-sonido.mjs on every pull request. Validated by the owner.

## sonido/sonidos-con-profundidad · Sounds with more depth
- File: sonido/sonidos-con-profundidad.md
- Depends on: power-ups, partida, enemigos/formacion

The synthesized sounds are replaced by eight recorded ones given by the owner (assets/sonidos, played from js/sonido.js): shot, enemy explosion, yellow ship explosion, player explosion, attack sound while ships dive, power-up pickup, a tune at the start of each wave and background music that speeds up, with rising pitch, from 100% to 200% as the wave's ships are destroyed, and goes silent while the player ship is destroyed.
