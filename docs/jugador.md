## Summary
The ship the player controls at the bottom of the screen: it moves horizontally with the arrow keys, by sliding a finger or by tilting the phone (jugador/accelerometer-control), fires with the space bar or a short tap on the touch screen, one shot at a time (the shot is freed when it hits a ship, in formation, diving or in the challenge wave, when it hits the mothership boss (enemigos/jefe), which stops every shot, the deep one included, or when it leaves through the top; the active power-up changes this), and explodes if a ship or a bomb hits it. It does not respawn by itself: Game makes it appear in the center when the game starts and with each spare ship, and outside a game it is not there. When it appears it blinks for two seconds and is invulnerable meanwhile (jugador/escudo-al-aparecer). When it fires, the cannon gives a brief flash (jugador/destello-disparo). When it explodes it throws out fragments that scatter and fade out (jugador/explosion-fragmentos). It accelerates and brakes smoothly, at 90 pixels per second at most with the arrows and 120 on a phone (jugador/inercia). While it moves, a small flame flickers under it (jugador/estela-motor). On an Android phone, the device vibrates when the ship explodes and when it picks up a power-up capsule (jugador/vibracion). Improvement pending in a child draft: shake on death. A shot that leaves through the top without hitting anything, or the ship exploding, breaks the player's combo (marcador/combos). In a two-player game (multijugador) there are two ships, J1 and J2 in different colours, each with its own power-up, spare ships and points.
