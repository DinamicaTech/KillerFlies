## Summary
Idea under debate: after every wave, a challenge wave in which the alien ships cross the screen in groups, in patterns and without firing, as in Galaga. Destroying all of them earns an extra life (one more spare ship); the challenge ends as soon as a ship leaves the screen, since the life can no longer be won, and it lasts about 20 seconds at most (the time the last group takes to cross). In a two-player game the ships destroyed by both count together, and if they succeed each player gets one life.
