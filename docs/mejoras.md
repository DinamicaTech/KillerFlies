## Summary
Permanent upgrades bought with coins, to motivate the player to go further in order to power up the ship. Built in phases: coins and the base shop (phases 1 and 2) are done; phase 3, mejoras/escudo-y-apoyo (shield, support ships and the paid bonus screen), is done; phase 4, mejoras/skins (skins, cosmetics and the remaining upgrades), is built and pending validation; phase 5, mejoras/cuentas (player profiles, up to 4 per difficulty), is built and pending validation; the rest is the draft child mejoras/dos-jugadores (coins and upgrades in the two-player game).
Coins, in js/monedas.js (KF.monedas): in a one-player game each main wave has 5 coins and each can be collected only once. At the start of a wave the coins still missing in it are planned: all but one drop from random enemies of the formation and the last one always from the last enemy destroyed. A coin falls like a power-up capsule and is picked up by touching it with the ship; one that escapes at the bottom stays pending for a later game. The mothership boss has no coins (the challenge wave has its own, mejoras/escudo-y-apoyo), and in a two-player game none drop. What is collected is saved in the browser at once, under the key killerflies.cuenta.default (one key per account, ready for mejoras/cuentas), apart for each difficulty: the balance, the coins collected in each wave and the bought upgrades. The scoreboard shows the balance at the bottom left; picking up a coin plays the power-up sound.
Shop, in js/tienda.js (KF.tienda): the 'SHOP' option of the pause menu, under 'CONTINUE', in one-player games. A shop appears next to the ship and a little pilot walks from the ship to it (1.5 s); then the list opens with the balance: SPEED (+5% ship speed per level, 5 coins), FIRE (+5% shot speed per level, so it fires more often, 5), SHIPS (one more spare ship at the start of each game, up to the cap of 5, so 3 levels, 15), BOMBS (enemy bombs at 95% of their speed per level, 5), MAGNET (coins and capsules near the ship drift toward it, 10, once) and RED SLOW (the slow fire capsule falls in red, 3, once), then BACK. Under the list, the marked row's explanation is shown in one or two lines (for example '+5% SHIP SPEED PER LEVEL'). Up and down choose, Enter or space (on release) or a tap buy; without enough coins it says how many are missing ('NEED n MORE COINS'). BACK walks the pilot back and returns to the pause menu; P closes the shop and resumes. Upgrades are permanent, count only for the difficulty whose coins bought them, apply at once (SHIPS from the next game) and do not count in a two-player game. Stackable upgrades have no cap other than SHIPS's: balance comes from the prices and playtesting.
Testing backdoor: opening the game with `?monedas=N` adds N coins to the balance of the active profile of every difficulty, once (the parameter is removed from the address).
Tested by tools/comprobar-mejoras.mjs in the "Pruebas" workflow.
**Acceptance:** play a NORMAL game and pick up coins in wave 1; pause, choose SHOP and see the pilot walk to the shop and the list with the balance; buy SPEED and feel the ship faster; start another game: wave 1 only drops the coins still missing; switch to EASY: its balance is 0. Validated by the owner by playing (reached wave 9 with only FIRE upgrades and it was still a challenge).
